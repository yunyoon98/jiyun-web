Selected
research

Four lines of work, from continental-scale movement of avian influenza to the sample preparation that makes those genomes readable in the first place.

  1. 01

    Intercontinental movement of avian influenza viruses
    • Analyzed PB2, PB1, PA, HA, NP, NA, M, and NS segments from global avian influenza datasets to identify intercontinental movement events.
    • Characterized the geographic origin, timing, and direction of movement, and evaluated the contribution of host groups.
    • Assessed ecological correlates of viral movement, including temporal patterns and temperature-related factors.
  2. 02

    Asia-wide genotyping of HPAI H5 clade 2.3.4.4b — GenoFLU-Asia
    • Performed phylogenetic analysis of all eight genomic segments of HPAI H5 clade 2.3.4.4b viruses detected across Asia from 2020 to 2026.
    • Characterized 131 genotype constellations circulating in Asian countries based on whole-genome segment combinations.
    • Adapted the GenoFLU framework and reference structure for Asia-focused genotype classification, supporting regional interpretation of HPAI genomic diversity.
    Visit GenoFLU-Asia ↗ View on GitHub ↗
  3. 03

    Sequence-based analysis of seasonal influenza vaccine antigenic-site mismatch
    • Compared HA antigenic-site sequences of circulating A(H1N1)pdm09, A(H3N2), and B/Victoria viruses with the corresponding seasonal vaccine strains.
    • Evaluated temporal changes in antigenic-site mismatch, observing increasing sequence divergence from vaccine strains across multiple periods.
    • Identified pronounced mismatch patterns associated with the COVID-19 pandemic period and subsequent seasonal influenza circulation.
  4. 04

    Viral Enrichment and Metagenomic/Whole-genome Sequencing
    • Evaluated sample pretreatment and viral enrichment approaches for clinical and cell-culture-derived samples to raise the proportion of viral reads and sequencing depth.
    • Performed metagenomic analysis, de novo assembly, reference mapping, and whole-genome sequence reconstruction for viral genomic characterization.
