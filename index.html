I am a Ph.D. student in the Laboratory of Veterinary Microbiology at the College of Veterinary Medicine, Kyungpook National University.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of phylogenetics, phylodynamics, and whole-genome sequencing. I have reconstructed the introduction and spread of clade 2.3.4.4b highly pathogenic avian influenza in South Korea, traced intercontinental movement of avian influenza viruses between Eurasia and North America, and built an Asia-wide genotyping framework from all eight genomic segments.

Alongside the computational work I work at the bench: virus isolation in embryonated chicken eggs, RT-PCR and qPCR, viral genome enrichment, NGS library preparation, and avian pathogenicity studies.
